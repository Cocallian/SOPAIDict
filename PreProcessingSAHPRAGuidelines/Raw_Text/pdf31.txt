Guideline for the Application of a License to Install, Use, 
Operate, and Apply for Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) 
The purpose of this document is to guide applicants seeking a license to install and use Magnetic Resonance 
Imaging (MRI) equipment. It outlines the necessary processes involved in applying for a license, the required 
supporting documents, and safety requirements. This guideline represents the minimum standards set forth 
by the South African Health Products Regulatory Authority (SAHPRA) for submitting applications to install and 
use MRI equipment. 
 
MRI systems are regulated under the Hazardous Substances Act (Act 15 of 1973) and its subsequent 
regulations. According to Section 4(c) of the Hazardous Substances Act, any individual or company wishing to 
use, operate, apply, install, or store an MRI machine—whether for medical or non-medical purposes—must 
apply for a license in accordance with this guide. Before an MRI system can be installed and used, it must be 
licensed for import or manufacturing, and the application for technical evaluation will only be considered 
afterward. There is a separate guideline for obtaining a license to import listed electronic products, as 
described in Regulation 1302 of the schedule for listed electronic products. 
 
The application form for obtaining a license to install and use MRI equipment can be found on the SAHPRA 
website.

1. 
INTRODUCTION 
The installation, use, and operation of Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) machines are regulated by the 
Radiation Control Unit of the South African Health Products Authority, in accordance with the Hazardous 
Substances Act 15 of 1973. MRI machines are listed under section 5.11 of Regulation 1302, which includes a 
schedule of electronic products that are controlled and regulated for use. This also covers the installation, 
application, and maintenance of these devices, as detailed in section 4 of the Hazardous Substances Act. 
MRI machines pose risks of exposing the public to strong magnetic fields and radiofrequency (RF) waves. These 
risks can lead to issues such as the malfunctioning of implanted medical devices, burns from heating, and 
projectile hazards from ferromagnetic objects, among others. 
To minimize these risks, the Radiation Control Unit of the South African Health Products Authority aims to 
ensure compliance with the regulations and safety requirements surrounding the use, operation, and 
application of MRI machines. 
1.1 
Purpose 
The purpose of this guideline is to ensure that the installation and use of MRI equipment comply with safety 
regulations. Additionally, the guideline aims to improve and enhance public safety by minimizing exposure 
to magnetic fields during the operation and application of MRI equipment. 
1.2 
Scope 
To assist the applicant in applying for a license to install, use and operate MRI equipment, including the 
required supporting documents and necessary safety regulations to that must be followed.

3. 
APPLICATION PROCESS  
3.1 
 Application Form 
Any individual or entity wishing to install, use, operate, or apply MRI equipment for either medical or non-
medical purposes must apply for a license through the Radiation Control Unit of SAHPRA. To initiate this 
process, 
applicants 
must 
complete 
the 
prescribed 
application 
form 
found 
on 
the 
link 
https://www.sahpra.org.za/radiation-control-application-and-report-forms/.   
The completed, signed, and dated application form should be submitted in PDF format to the email address 
nirmed.application@sahpra.org.za. It is essential that the application clearly includes the following 
information: 
a) The physical address of the premises (Hospital or Diagnostic practice) 
b) Contact details of the person responsible (including telephone and email address) 
c) Information about the reporting radiologist (including the latest HPSCA number) 
d) Details of qualified MRI radiographers (including the latest HPSCA number) 
e) A description of the MRI equipment (including model and Tesla levels) 
f) Serial Number of the machine (It must be provided to the regulator when machine is delivered, if not 
available when application is sent) 
g) The product model import license number (which must be provided by the suppliers)  
3.2

of SAHPRA using the email address nirmed.application@sahpra.rog.za accompanied by the following 
documentation: 
a. Professional registration supporting documentation

4. 
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AND REJECTION OF THE APPLICATION  
a. Upon receipt of a completed application form for a license to install and use MRI, including all 
required supporting documents, the applicant will receive an acknowledgment confirming the 
receipt of the application.  
b. The model of the MRI machine applied must be licensed for importation. 
c. The application will be subjected to the screening, evaluation, and approval processes with a 
timeframe of 60 working days. 
d. After the technical evaluation of the application, the applicant will be sent an email informing 
them to proceed with the installation. 
e. Depending on the technical evaluation results of the submitted supporting documents, the 
regulator might request to perform the pre inspections. 
f. 
When the application is approved, the Regulator will inform the applicant through an email  
g. If the application came through with incorrect or incomplete supporting documents, the 
Regulator would send an email to the applicant requesting the complete/correct documentation 
to be submitted. 
h. The applicant will have five (10) working days to submit the correct supporting documents from 
the day they receive the email from Regulator about incorrect or complete documents. 
i. 
If the applicant has not responded to the Regulator with correct supporting documents within 
the allocated timeframe, they will receive a reminder.

5. 
VALIDITY AND RENEWAL OF THE USE LICENSE FOR MRI 
a. The use license of MRI will be valid for five (5) years.  
b. The license will be renewable within 90 (3 months) working days before the expiry date.  
c. The applicant must follow the steps in section 3 for submission of the application to renew. 
d. The renewal application will be subjected to the steps in Section 4. 
e. The applicant will be issued with communication confirming the submission of the application to 
renew and referred to technical evaluation and assessment. 
 
6. 
COMMISSIONING AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS OF MRI 
6.1 
Acceptance Tests  
The applicant must keep a file of acceptance tests that are performed as part of the installation and 
commissioning of MRI equipment in preparation of the equipment for clinical usage. The following 
minimum tests must be performed: 
a. Shielding tests,  
b. Electrical safety tests,  
c. Confirmation of the magnetic field 0.5mT (5 Gauss),  
d. Confirmation of the noise level among others  
The applicant or licence holder must arrange that their technical representative(s) are involved or present 
during the performance of the commissioning and acceptancy tests. 
6.2 
 Acceptance Tests by the applicant  
In addition to the tests performed by the supplier of the MRI equipment, an applicant must 
perform tests to ensure the following:

7. 
MRI SAFETY  
a. The licence holder will be responsible for ensuring the safety of the staff, patients, and public 
in and around the MRI premises.  
b. The licence holder must ensure that the MRI room has access control. 
c. The licence holder must ensure that the premises are correctly demarcated for access control. 
d. The licence holder must ensure that warning signs are clearly displayed at entrance.
