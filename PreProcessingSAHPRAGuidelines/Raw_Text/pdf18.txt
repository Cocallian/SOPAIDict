Introducing the SAHPRA Medicines Online Directory Phase 2  for Rx Medicines
SAHPRA launched the OTC Online Medicines Directory in June 2022. Please visit: 
www.medsinfo.sahpra.org.za
The purpose of the Online Medicines Directory is to provide patients, consumers, and healthcare 
providers with a single, trusted source of information for all medicines approved by SAHPRA. 
Phase 1 was to include all Schedule 0-2 products (OTCs). 
Phase 2 which is underway is to include all prescription only medicines (S3 and above). 
Once completed, the site will undergo an artwork change and have an OTC and Rx section.
The URL www.medsinfo.sahpra.org.za will stay the same. 
SAHPRA is working towards zero rating this website. 
SAHPRA Online Medicine Directory Rx Toolkit
This toolkit provides Market Authorisation Holders (MAH) activation guidance.
The toolkit provides essential creative guidelines and resources to ensure consistency and
alignment across all marketing and promotional platforms to effectively increase awareness and 
use of SAHPRA’s Online Medicines Directory to all healthcare professionals.
All Holders of Certificates of Registration are encouraged to develop a Standard Operating 
Procedure (SOP) for this process to ensure that the correct QR code is consistently assigned.

Key Visuals
Three visuals have been provided. One of these visuals must be included on all prescription-only 
promotional material, such as detail aids, professional journal advertisements, and similar items.
The first visual includes a QR code generated directly from the specific product or medicine on the 
site.
The second  features a QR code that links directly to the MedsInfo landing page.
The third provides only the URL link to the MedsInfo site.
If you are promoting a prescription only medicine, you must ideally incorporate the QR code 
specific to your product—generated from the backend system into which you have access —into 
the artwork. This artwork will then be integrated into your promotional material.
Once generated, your QR code remains unchanged, even when updates occur.
