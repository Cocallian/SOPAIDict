GUIDELINE FOR PREPARATION OF A SITE MASTER FILE (SMF) 
FOR WHOLESALERS OF MEDICAL DEVICES 
This document serves as a guideline to assist applicants to comply with the requirements for a Site Master 
File (SMF) regarding the wholesaling of medical devices (including In vitro diagnostics (IVDs)). The South 
African Health Products Regulatory Authority (SAHPRA) is committed to ensure that all premises where 
medical devices are procured and stored for the purposes of selling to retailers and end users are of the 
required quality, safety and performance, and that wholesaling activities are conducted comply with statutory 
requirements and complies with acceptable quality assurance principles and good wholesale practices as 
determined by the Authority.

In-Vitro Diagnostic medical device

First-In, First-Out - Means a system where the first medical device received is

Quality Assurance - Means the responsibility and activity of ensuring that the

QMS 
Quality Management System - Means collective policies, plans, practices, and 
the supporting infrastructure by which an organization aims to reduce and 
eventually eliminate non-conformances to specifications, standards, and 
customer expectations in the most cost effective and efficient manner. 
RUO  
Research Use Only 
SMF 
For the purpose of this document, a Site Master File is a document prepared by 
the medical device and IVD wholesaler and should contain specific information 
about the quality management policies and activities of the site, the quality 
control of wholesaling activities carried out at the named site and any closely 
integrated operations at adjacent and nearby buildings. 
Wholesaler 
Means a dealer who purchases a medical device from a licensed manufacturer 
or distributor and sells to a retailer or end user in terms of Section 22H of the 
Medicines and Related Substances Act, 101 of 1965.

1. INTRODUCTION 
The Site Master File (SMF) is a mandatory supporting document for a medical device establishment license 
application in accordance with section 22C of the Medicines and Related Substances Act (Act 101 of 1965).  
The Site Master File (SMF) is prepared by the wholesaler, and it contains specific information about the quality 
assurance and/or quality control of medical device wholesaling operations performed at the site and any 
closely integrated operations as adjacent and nearby buildings. A Quality Manual (QM) which is applicable to 
Manufacturers and Distributors (Importers) is also sufficient when submitting a medical device establishment 
licence application for wholesalers. 
1.1 Purpose 
This document guides medical device wholesaler applicants on preparation of a SMF. 
1.2 Scope 
This guideline is applicable for the preparation of a SMF that is used for application of medical device 
establishment license for wholesaler.  
This guideline is not applicable for the QM document required for application of medical device including IVDs 
establishment license for manufacturers and/or distributors (importers). 
2. LEGAL PROVISION 
a) Medicines and Related Substances Act, 101 of 1965, as amended 
b) Regulations relating to medical devices (including IVDs) 
c) Hazardous Substances Act, 15 of 1973, as amended 
3. REQUIREMENTS OF A SITE MASTER FILE 
3.1 General 
The SMF must have an issue number and an effective date, and must be updated when a major amendment 
or upgrade is made within the operations or when key activities and/or key personnel change.  
An updated SMF must be submitted for the renewal application of a medical device establishment licence 
every five years or for any amendment application thereof. Wherever possible, digitized plans or schematic 
layouts should be included instead of a narrative.

GUIDANCE

3.2.2 Qualifications, experience, and responsibilities of key personnel 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Brief details of academic qualifications, work-related experience, and years of relevant 
experience since qualifying. 
b) Provide a brief job description for key personnel. 
3.2.3 Outline of arrangements for basic and in-service training and how records are 
maintained 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Describe how training needs are identified and by whom. 
b) Provide details of training related to GWP requirements. 
c) State the form of training, e.g., in-house, external, and how practical experience is gained, 
and which staff are involved. 
d) Explain how the effectiveness of the training is assessed, e.g., by questionnaires. 
e) Explain how retraining needs are identified. 
f) Give brief details of the type of training records kept. 
3.2.4 Health and safety management requirements for personnel engaged in wholesaling 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Give brief details, e.g., name, designation, qualification, contact number of personnel 
responsible for ensuring the safety of employees.

3.3 PREMISES AND EQUIPMENT 
3.3.1 Site plan or description of wholesaling areas (size, location and immediate 
environment and other wholesaling activities on the site), with indication of scale 
(architectural or engineering drawings are not required) 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Provide a site plan indicating the location of the site and surrounding area as well as 
highlighting all wholesaling and other functional areas. 
b) Include a description of the site, the location and immediate environment and the size of 
the site and types of building 
c) Give a brief description of the controls available to prevent unauthorized access. 
d) Provide a floor plan of each area with an indication of scale. Label areas and annotate the 
plan with names. 
 
3.3.2 Brief description of ventilation systems (More detail should be given for critical areas 
providing special storage conditions) 
 
GUIDANCE 
Provide a brief description of ventilation systems with more detail for critical storage areas (provide 
schematic drawings). 
Include the following data: 
a) Specification of air supply 
b) Temperature 
c) Humidity

3.3.3 Special areas for the handling of highly toxic, hazardous, fridge-line and radio-active 
medical devices (including IVDs) 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Provide the same layout as above in Section 3.3.1 for the description of areas specifically 
designated for the handling of highly toxic, hazardous, fridge-line and radioactive medical 
devices. 
3.3.4 Site maintenance (description of planned preventive maintenance programmes and 
recording system) 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Describe the preventative maintenance programme. 
b) Mention the person responsible for the maintenance and servicing of equipment. 
c) Provide written procedures and contractual details for third-party service providers. 
d) Provide written procedures and suitable reporting forms for maintenance and servicing of 
wholesaling equipment. 
e) Mention the documentation process involved in recording the type and frequency of services 
and checks, including the details of service, repairs and modifications to wholesaling 
equipment. 
f) Describe and identify maintenance routines that could possibly affect the medical device 
including IVDs quality. 
3.3.5 Availability of written specifications and procedures for cleaning wholesaling areas and 
equipment 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Provide cleaning procedures for each area. 
b) Provide cleaning methods and frequencies used for delivery vehicles.

3.3.6 Policy on the storage of medical devices (including IVDs) 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Describe how medical devices (including IVDs) are segregated and controlled, e.g., by 
computer, labels, etc. 
b) Describe how medical devices are stored, e.g., pallet racking. 
c) Describe any special storage or handling conditions. 
d) Describe pest control management. 
3.4 STOCK HANDLING AND STOCK CONTROL 
3.4.1 Arrangements and recording system for wholesaling 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Description of receiving, handling and storage of medical devices: 
b) Inspection of medical devices (including IVDs) at point of receipt. 
c) Describe how batch/lot numbers and expiry dates are managed during the receipt and 
dispatch of stock. Additionally, describe the inventory management system method 
followed to ensure proper stock rotation. 
d) Describe the methods of distribution used to customers, e.g., post, courier, van services, 
customer collection, etc. 
e) Records of distribution: Describe how the retained records permit full batch traceability 
from the Original Equipment Manufacturer to the customer, in terms of the date of sale, 
customer details, and quantity dispatched. 
f) Describe stock-take procedures, including information on the manner and frequency in 
which inventory control is conducted.

3.4.2 Deliveries and transportation 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Description of how the security, storage conditions and protection of the quality of medical 
devices (including IVDs) are maintained during transportation. 
b) Description of the vehicle fleet available: 
c) Number of vehicles and their capacity 
d) Details of the vehicle specially adapted to transport special medical devices (e.g., cold items, 
radioactive medical devices) when applicable. 
e) How the transport route is planned (e.g., cold items, radioactive medical devices). 
3.5 DOCUMENTATION 
3.5.1 Arrangements for the preparation, revision, storage, and distribution of necessary 
documentation for wholesaling activities. Any other documentation related to product 
quality which is not mentioned elsewhere. 
 
GUIDANCE 
Arrangements for the preparation, revision and distribution of documentation: 
a) Provide a description of the documentation system. 
b) State the personnel responsible for the preparation, revision, and distribution of 
documentation. 
c) Specify where the master documents will be stored. 
d) State the standard format and instructions on how documents are to be prepared. 
e) Describe how documentation is controlled. 
f) State the period for keeping the documents (e.g. five years etc). 
g) Detail any arrangements for electronic records.

3.6 MEDICAL DEVICE COMPLAINTS AND FIELD SAFETY CORRECTIVE ACTIONS 
3.6.1 System for the handling of complaints of medical devices 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Describe the procedure for medical device complaints. 
b) State the personnel responsible for logging, classifying, and investigating complaints. 
c) State if written reports of complaints will be prepared and the personnel responsible for 
reviewing the reports. 
d) State the period for keeping the records of complaints (e.g. five years etc) 
3.6.2 Arrangements for handling of field safety corrective actions 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) State the written procedures that are in place in the following processes: 
b) Retrieval of distribution data. 
c) Notification of customers. 
d) Receipt/segregation/inspection of returned medical devices. 
e) Investigation and reporting of complaints and adverse events. 
f) Reporting corrective action. 
g) State the personnel responsible for coordinating field safety corrective actions. 
h) State who should notify the Authority of field safety corrective action.

3.7 INTERNAL AUDITS 
3.7.1 Short description of the internal audit system 
 
GUIDANCE 
a) Describe how the internal audit system verifies that those activities that have a bearing on 
medical device quality comply with the planned arrangement. 
b) State documented procedures that are in place for self-inspection and any applicable follow-
up actions. 
c) State whether the results of the internal audit will be documented and brought to the 
attention of the personnel responsible for the areas and activities that are being inspected. 
d) State whether corrective actions will be taken to address deficiencies found in the audit in 
a timely manner.
