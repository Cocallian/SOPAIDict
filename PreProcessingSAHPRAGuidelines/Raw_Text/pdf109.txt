manufactured, stored or tested are of good standard and that all premises where pharmaceutical business is conducted
comply with statutory requirements. Applicants must ensure that all administrative requirements are adhered to.

1.
INTRODUCTION
The Site Master File is prepared by the manufacturer and or wholesaler and contains specific information about
the quality assurance, the production and/or quality control of pharmaceutical manufacturing operations and
or wholesaling operations carried out at the named site and any closely integrated operations at adjacent and
nearby buildings. If only part of a pharmaceutical operation is carried out on the site, a Site Master File need
only describe those operations, e.g., analysis, packaging, etc.
When submitted to a regulatory authority, the Site Master File provides information on the manufacturer's
operations or the wholesaler’s operations and procedures that can be useful in the efficient planning and
undertaking of a GMP or GWP inspection.
These guidance notes have been set out in such a manner that each chapter and the paragraphs noted under
"REQUIREMENT" is followed by "GUIDANCE" to provide details of how the requirements should be interpreted.
The Site Master File should have an edition number and an effective date. The Site Master file must be updated
when a major constructional upgrade takes place or when key activities and or key personnel, changes. In
addition, an updated Site Master File must be submitted to the office of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) every
five years.
1.1
Purpose
The purpose of this guideline is to guide the manufacturer or wholesaler of medicinal products in the
preparation of a Site Master File that can be useful to the regulatory authority in planning and conducting
GMP or GWP inspections.
1.2
Scope
The scope of this document applies to the preparation of the Site Master File. Refer to national regulatory
requirements to establish whether it is mandatory for manufacturers or wholesalers of medicinal products
to prepare a Site Master File.
2.
LEGAL PROVISION
This guideline was prepared with reference to Medicines and Related Substances Act, 1965 (Act 101 of 1965).
