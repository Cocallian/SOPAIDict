1. 
INTRODUCTION 
The use of Group IV Hazardous Substance is restricted by R247 to radiation workers with basic principles of 
radiation protection and safety relevant to their role, in order to limit overexposure caused by human error. 
1.1 
Purpose 
To guide the relevant authority holders involved in the decision to subject radiation workers to radiation to 
make informed and correct request for such examination, thereby protecting the radiation workers from 
unnecessary exposure to ionizing radiation. 
1.2 
Scope 
This guideline is applicable to the relevant authority holders involved in requisitions for medical 
examination for radiation workers to ensure their protection from unnecessary exposure to ionizing 
radiation. 
2. 
LEGAL PROVISION 
The SAHPRA Radiation control regulates ionizing devices as promulgated by the Hazardous Substances Act 15 
of 1973 with its related regulations. 
3. 
REQUEST FOR MEDICAL EXAMINATIONS 
3.1 
Guidelines for requests 
3.1.1 
Before any person is appointed as a radiation worker, (s)he must undergo a medical examination. 
3.1.2 
Medical examinations for radiation workers should follow general occupational medical practice 
for determining fitness for work. 
3.1.3 
Each radiation worker will be required to undergo a medical examination in the event of the 
following: 
3.1.3.1 When a radiation occurrence/ incident resulting in an abnormally high dose is suspected to 
have taken place or has been confirmed. 
3.1.3.2 When a medical practitioner deems it necessary. 
3.1.3.3 When such an examination is considered necessary either by the regulatory authority or 
the holder of the authority, and 
3.1.3.4 When the radiation worker suspects that his/ her health has been, or will be, adversely
