1. INTRODUCTION 
The South African Health Products Regulatory Authority (SAHPRA) is mandated, in terms of the Hazardous 
Substances Act, 1973 (Act No. 15 of 1973) ("the Act") and the Regulations promulgated thereunder, to 
regulate the importation, sale, letting, use and continued compliance of listed electronic products, including 
electromedical devices. 
In fulfilment of this mandate, SAHPRA requires all Licence Holders of licensed electromedical devices to 
demonstrate continued compliance with the conditions of their licences through the annual submission of 
current regulatory and conformity assessment information. 
Licence Condition 9 forms an integral part of every licence issued in respect of an electromedical device and 
requires Licence Holders to maintain current conformity assessment documentation and to provide such 
information to SAHPRA upon request or during the annual compliance review process. 
The Annual Submission of Compliance Information (ASOCI) process enables SAHPRA to verify the continued 
validity of conformity assessment documentation, regulatory certification and other supporting information 
to ensure that licensed electromedical devices continue to comply with the applicable legislative and 
regulatory requirements throughout the validity period of the licence. 
 
1.1 PURPOSE 
The purpose of this Guideline is to prescribe the regulatory requirements, administrative procedures and 
compliance obligations applicable to Licence Holders required to complete and submit the Annual 
Submission of Compliance Information Form (OF-RDN-NM-06A), which replaces the legacy Form 41BM-2. 
This Guideline establishes a uniform administrative framework for the annual submission, assessment and 
maintenance of compliance information relating to licensed electromedical devices and provides guidance on 
the regulatory actions that may arise during the annual compliance review process. 
More specifically, this Guideline sets out the requirements relating to: 
• 
the annual distribution of the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form; 
• 
the submission of annual compliance information by Licence Holders;

1.2 SCOPE 
This Guideline applies to every Licence Holder holding one or more valid licences issued by SAHPRA in 
respect of electromedical devices to which Licence Condition 9 applies. 
The provisions of this Guideline are applicable to: 
• 
importers of listed electromedical devices; 
• 
Licence Holders authorised to import, sell, let, distribute or otherwise place licensed electromedical 
devices on the South African market; 
• 
authorised regulatory representatives acting on behalf of Licence Holders; 
• 
persons responsible for maintaining regulatory documentation relating to licensed electromedical 
devices; and 
• 
SAHPRA personnel responsible for the administration, assessment, processing, maintenance and 
oversight of licensing and compliance records. 
This Guideline applies irrespective of whether the licensed electromedical device has been certified under 
the applicable European Medical Device Directives or Regulation (EU) 2017/745 on Medical Devices (MDR), 
provided that the conformity assessment documentation remains valid and is recognised under the 
applicable legislative and regulatory framework.

3. COMPLETION OF THE ANNUAL SUBMISSION OF COMPLIANCE INFORMATION 
FORM (OF-RDN-NM-06A) 
Licence Holders shall complete and submit the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form (OF-
RDN-NM-06A) in accordance with the requirements prescribed in this Guideline. 
The information submitted shall be complete, accurate, current and supported by the applicable conformity 
assessment documentation and any additional information that may be required by SAHPRA for the 
purposes of regulatory assessment. 
The submission of incomplete, inaccurate, misleading or false information, or the failure to submit the 
required information within the prescribed timeframe, may constitute non-compliance with the conditions of 
the licence and may result in regulatory action in accordance with the Hazardous Substances Act, 1973 (Act 
No. 15 of 1973), the applicable Regulations and the conditions of the licence. 
Licence Holders shall ensure that all information submitted to SAHPRA remains current throughout the 
assessment process and shall promptly notify SAHPRA of any material changes affecting the information 
previously submitted. 
The following instructions shall be followed when completing the Annual Submission of Compliance

Submission Format 
The Annual Submission of Compliance Information process shall be conducted as follows: 
• 
SAHPRA shall issue the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form (OF-RDN-NM-06A) to 
the Licence Holder in Microsoft Excel format. 
• 
The Licence Holder shall complete the prescribed form in full and submit the completed form to 
SAHPRA in Portable Document Format (PDF). 
• 
The completed form shall be submitted electronically to the designated SAHPRA email address, 
Radcon.Asoci@sahpra.org.za. 
• 
Forms that are incomplete, submitted in an incorrect format, or submitted together with 
unsolicited supporting documentation may be returned to the Licence Holder for correction prior 
to assessment.

4.2 Distribution of the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form 
4.2.1 SAHPRA shall issue the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form (OF-RDN-NM-06A, 
replacing Old Form: 41BM-2) electronically to the designated contact person of each Licence Holder within 
the applicable compliance group. 
4.2.2 Distribution of the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form shall commence annually from 
1 April and continue in accordance with the established compliance schedule for each respective compliance 
group. 
4.2.3 The electronic notification shall specify the submission requirements, the prescribed submission period 
and any additional information necessary to facilitate compliance. 
4.3 Submission Period 
Licence Holders shall submit a duly completed Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form, together 
with any supporting documentation requested by SAHPRA, within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of 
the electronic notification. 
 
5. ASSESSMENT OF THE ANNUAL SUBMISSION OF COMPLIANCE INFORMATION 
5.1 Receipt of Submissions 
Completed Annual Submission of Compliance Information Forms shall be submitted to the designated 
SAHPRA electronic mailbox from which the original notification was issued.

5.2 Assessment of Submissions 
Upon receipt, submissions shall be acknowledged and allocated to the responsible regulatory scientist for 
assessment. 
5.3 Requests for Additional Information 
Where the submitted information is incomplete or requires clarification, SAHPRA may request additional 
information or supporting documentation from the Licence Holder. The Licence Holder shall provide the 
requested information within the period specified by SAHPRA. 
5.4 Updating of Compliance Status 
Upon completion of the assessment, the Annual Submission of Compliance Information (ASOCI) status of 
each licence shall be updated accordingly within the SAHPRA regulatory records. 
5.5 Processing Timeframes 
Subject to the submission of a complete and satisfactory Annual Submission of Compliance Information 
Form, SAHPRA will endeavour to complete the assessment process within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt 
 
6. FAILURE TO SUBMIT THE ANNUAL SUBMISSION OF COMPLIANCE INFORMATION 
6.1 Notice of Non-Compliance 
Where a Licence Holder fails to submit the completed Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form 
within the prescribed submission period, or where the submission is incomplete, SAHPRA shall issue a 
written Notice of Non-Compliance to the Licence Holder. 
6.2 Opportunity to Remedy the Non-Compliance 
The Notice of Non-Compliance shall afford the Licence Holder a period of five (5) working days within which 
to submit the outstanding information or otherwise remedy the non-compliance.

7.1 Regulatory Action 
Where a Licence Holder fails to remedy the identified non-compliance within the prescribed period, SAHPRA 
may, subject to the provisions of the Medicines and Related Substances Act, 1965 (Act 101 of 1965), the 
Hazardous Substances Act, 1973 (Act No. 15 of 1973), and the applicable Regulations, cancel the licence(s) 
relating to the affected medical device(s). 
7.2 Notification of Cancellation 
Following cancellation of the relevant licence(s), SAHPRA shall issue a formal Notice of Cancellation to the 
affected Licence Holder. 
7.3 Submission of New Applications 
Where a licence has been cancelled as a result of non-compliance, the Licence Holder shall be required to 
submit a new licence application for each affected medical device in accordance with the applicable 
legislative and regulatory requirements. 
 
8. ISSUANCE OF UPDATED LICENCE DOCUMENTS 
8.1 Updated Licence Documents 
Following completion of the annual compliance assessment and the implementation of any resulting 
regulatory actions, SAHPRA shall issue updated licence documents to the relevant Licence Holders. 
8.2 Content of Updated Licence Documents 
The updated licence document shall reflect the current licensing status of the Licence Holder, including any 
amendments arising from the annual compliance assessment. 
8.3 Timeframe 
Subject to the approval of the Annual Submission of Compliance Information Form, SAHPRA will endeavour 
to issue updated licence documents within thirty (30) calendar days following completion of the assessment.

11. VALIDITY 
This Guideline shall remain valid for a period of five (5) years from its effective date and supersedes the 
previous 41BM-2A – ASOCI Guidelines and Corrections. The Guideline shall be reviewed upon expiry of its 
validity period or earlier where amendments to the applicable legislation, regulatory requirements, or 
SAHPRA operational processes necessitate such review.
